Privacy policy

This policy applies to Učimo se slovenščino, package com.saidf.ucimoslovenscino.

Effective date: 10 September 2026

1. Who operates the app

Učimo se slovenščino is an independent educational application published by Saifex. It is not an official language examination body and does not issue official CEFR certificates.

2. Learning data stored on your device

The app stores your selected interface language, completed lessons, best scores, passed level checks and items scheduled for review. This information remains locally on your device. We do not provide user accounts or synchronize learning progress to our own server.

3. Advertising and consent

The production version may use Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your device, region and consent choices, Google may process approximate location derived from an IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform asks for consent before the app requests ads. You can reopen advertising privacy choices from the app menu when this option is available. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily remove all ads; it changes how eligible ads are selected.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads disclosure.

4. Audio and speech

The app uses prerecorded lesson audio when available and may use the Slovene text-to-speech voice installed on your device. The app does not request microphone permission, record your voice or upload voice recordings. Your operating system or selected text-to-speech provider may process the Slovene text needed to produce speech under its own terms.

5. Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Because there is no app account or cloud progress profile, there is no remote learning profile for us to delete. Data processed by Google is governed by Google's retention and deletion practices.

6. Your choices and rights

You can reset local learning data through Android application settings, remove all local data by uninstalling the app, and change eligible advertising choices through the privacy options shown in the app. Privacy questions or requests can be sent through the support channel below.

7. Children

The app is a general language-learning product and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The intended store audience is teenagers and adults. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own servers.

8. Security and policy changes

Network communication used by third-party services is encrypted in transit. We may update this policy when app functionality, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated after a material revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or application support, email saifex.apps@outlook.com or use the public support page. Do not include passwords, identity documents, health information or other sensitive information in a public issue.
